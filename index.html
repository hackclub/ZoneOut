What is ZoneOut about?
ZoneOut is a YSWS where you create something themed around horror, mystery or ARG!
What is the theme?
Horror, mystery and ARG focusing on immersiveness and storytelling. [and, of course, good art!]
What kind of projects are to be submitted?
Projects can be:

• A website
• A terminal / CLI program
• A game
• A mod (eg. Minecraft)
Basically, anything that can be spooky.
This event is entirely software!
How do I track time?
Use Hackatime. HackClub's own time-tracking tool!
There's a shop?
Once you submit a project, you will be given an hour balance based on your approved hours to spend in the shop where you can buy things as part your "rewards"!
PS: To track your hours, use Hackatime!
What is Hack Club?
Hack Club is a non-profit global community for teenagers who like building crazy projects. The whole point is to make, learn, share, and have fun while doing it!
You can join our Slack community here!
What is the age requirement?
Hack Club events are open to ages 13-18 worldwide!
